Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 2026 (Updated)

1. Data Collection and Usage

Kineticue is deeply committed to your privacy. Our core philosophy is local-first: by default, all video processing, editing, and analysis are performed entirely on your local device. We do not automatically upload your personal videos to any external servers.

2. Cloud Storage for Shared Content (Optional)

If you explicitly choose to use features like "Target Clip Reuse" or community sharing, specific assets (including video segments, thumbnails, and analysis metadata) will be uploaded to and stored on our secure cloud servers (Google Firebase Storage). This is necessary to:

  • Allow other authenticated users to download and use the clip as a reference for comparative analysis.
  • Provide a synchronized experience across your authorized devices.

By enabling these sharing options, you acknowledge and consent to the cloud storage and distribution of the selected assets within the Kineticue ecosystem.

3. Authentication and Account Data

To provide personalized 서비스 such as membership management and data synchronization, we collect minimal identifiers when you create an account, including:

  • Email address and Display Name (provided via Google, Apple, or Email login)
  • Unique User ID (UID) generated by Firebase Authentication

This data is stored securely using Google Firebase and is used solely for the purpose of managing your account and service entitlements.

4. Third-Party Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personally identifiable information to outside parties. Your data remains yours. Shared "Target Clips" are only accessible to other registered users of the Service.

5. Account and Data Deletion

We respect your right to be forgotten. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time directly within the app (Profile > Delete Account). Upon deletion, your authentication records, personalized settings, and any videos uploaded to our cloud storage will be permanently removed.

6. Children's Privacy

Kineticue does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.
