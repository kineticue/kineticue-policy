Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 2026

1. Data Collection and Cloud Services

A. Local Storage: Most of your raw video analysis data is stored primarily on your local device.

B. Optional Cloud Services (Opt-In & Tier-Based):

  • Dashboard Consistency (All Users): To provide a consistent item dashboard across your devices, basic item metadata and thumbnail images are automatically synced to our secure cloud upon creation.
  • Profile Customization (Optional): If you choose to upload a custom profile image, it is stored securely in our cloud and displayed publicly within the app community to identify your content (e.g., on shared Feed or Target Clips).
  • Multi-Device Video Sync (Paid Feature): Uploading raw video files and background blur assets to our secure cloud is restricted to specific paid subscription tiers. This data remains private and is never shared.
  • Target Clips: Shared publicly within the app community solely at your explicit discretion.

2. YouTube API Services

Using the YouTube upload feature subjects you to the YouTube Terms of Service (link) and Google Privacy Policy (link).

3. Data Retention and Deletion Policy

A. Item Deletion: When you delete a synced item from the app, it is temporarily held in a secure "Soft Deleted" state on our servers for a grace period of up to 30 days to prevent accidental data loss. After this period, it is permanently and irreversibly purged from our secure cloud servers.

B. Account Deletion: Initiating account deletion or withdrawal will immediately and permanently delete all your cloud-stored videos, thumbnails, metadata, and user profile data from our secure cloud servers, bypassing any grace periods.

4. Subscription Data

We process minimal information required to manage your subscription tier and entitlement status through Google Play and Apple App Store systems.
